# Launch Posts

## GitHub Release Announcement

OpenVoice AI v0.1.0 is live.

It is an early preview of a self-hosted AI communication platform starting with:

- AI phone-agent baseline
- Asterisk integration examples
- local STT/TTS with faster-whisper and Piper
- dashboard foundation
- call recording and transcript workflow
- email draft foundations
- NVIDIA/OpenRouter provider config
- Proxmox LXC helper-script drafts

The goal is not just another chatbot. The goal is a self-hosted platform for phone, email, calendar, chat, automations and AI providers.

Repo: https://github.com/nikpottbecker/openvoice-ai

Status: preview / early alpha. Feedback is welcome, especially around STT quality, Proxmox installs and dashboard operations.

## LinkedIn

I just published the first preview of OpenVoice AI.

OpenVoice AI is an open-source, self-hosted AI communication platform. It starts with a phone-agent stack and is designed to grow into a unified system for phone, email, calendar, chat, automations and AI providers.

The v0.1.0 preview includes:

- Asterisk phone-agent baseline
- local speech-to-text and text-to-speech foundations
- dashboard and call history foundations
- call recording and transcript workflow
- email draft foundations
- NVIDIA and OpenRouter provider configuration
- Proxmox LXC helper-script drafts

It is still early alpha. The biggest current focus is improving STT quality for real phone calls and making installation easier for self-hosting users.

GitHub: https://github.com/nikpottbecker/openvoice-ai

Feedback from self-hosting, Asterisk, Proxmox and AI automation people would help a lot.
