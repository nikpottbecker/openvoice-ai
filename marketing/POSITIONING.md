# Positioning

## One-Liner

OpenVoice AI is a self-hosted AI communication platform for phone calls, email, dashboards, automations and AI providers.

## Short Pitch

OpenVoice AI starts with a self-hosted AI phone agent and grows toward a full communication platform: phone, email, calendar, chat, automations, local STT/TTS and multiple AI providers. It is built for people who want AI communication workflows without handing every call, transcript and customer message to a closed SaaS platform.

## Audience

- self-hosting users
- Proxmox homelab users
- small businesses
- photographers, agencies and freelancers
- makers building AI assistants
- privacy-conscious teams
- Asterisk/SIP users
- automation users with n8n or Home Assistant

## Core Promise

Own your AI communication stack.

## Differentiators

- self-hosted first
- phone-agent baseline, not just chat
- local STT/TTS support
- Asterisk and FRITZ!Box-friendly direction
- dashboard-first operations view
- open provider strategy: NVIDIA, OpenRouter and future local LLMs
- built around privacy and explicit approval for customer emails

## Honest Status

OpenVoice AI is currently v0.1.0 preview / early alpha.

Strong enough to show:

- architecture
- phone-agent baseline
- dashboard direction
- call recording and transcript workflow
- provider strategy

Still needs work:

- STT quality for real German phone calls
- setup wizard

- automated tests
- production-grade plugin interfaces
