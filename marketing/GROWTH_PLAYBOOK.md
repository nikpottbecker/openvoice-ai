# Growth Playbook

## Positioning Goal

OpenVoice AI should become known as:

> The self-hosted AI communication stack for people who want to own their calls, transcripts, email workflows and automations.

## Audience Segments

### Self-Hosters

Message:

Run your own AI phone and communication stack without depending on a closed SaaS platform.

Channels:

- r/selfhosted
- r/homelab
- Awesome Selfhosted lists
- Proxmox community

### Small Businesses

Message:

Capture calls, summarize requests and prepare follow-ups without manually writing notes after every conversation.

Channels:

- LinkedIn
- local business networks
- agency communities

### Developers

Message:

Open-source platform for phone, email, calendar, automation and AI provider integrations.

Channels:

- Hacker News
- GitHub
- Discord/Telegram dev groups
- n8n community

### VoIP / Asterisk Users

Message:

Bring AI workflows into SIP/Asterisk setups while keeping telephony under your control.

Channels:

- Asterisk forums
- FreePBX community
- VoIP Reddit

## GitHub Growth Tasks

- Add screenshots with synthetic data.
- Add demo video or GIF.
- Add `good first issue` labels.
- Add 5-10 contributor-friendly issues.
- Add architecture diagram to README.
- Add public STT benchmark document.
- Add “Known limitations” section.
- Add “Why OpenVoice AI?” comparison document.

## Content Ideas

- Why self-hosted AI phone agents are hard.
- Benchmarking German telephone STT.
- Asterisk + AI: what works and what breaks.
- How to protect an AI dashboard with Cloudflare Access.
- Building an open-source alternative to closed AI receptionist tools.

## Star Growth Without Spam

- Launch early but honestly.
- Ask for technical feedback, not stars.
- Build in public.
- Reply quickly to issues.
- Tag releases clearly.
- Show screenshots and real progress.
- Publish benchmark results even when they are imperfect.
