# Website Copy

## Hero

OpenVoice AI

The Open-Source AI Communication Platform.

Self-host phone calls, email workflows, dashboards, automations and AI providers in one privacy-first communication stack.

## Value Props

- Own your communication data.
- Start with an AI phone agent.
- Use local STT/TTS where possible.
- Connect Asterisk, email, calendar and automations.
- Keep humans in control of outbound customer messages.

## Sections

### Phone

Answer calls, record audio, transcribe conversations, summarize intent and route follow-up actions.

### Dashboard

Monitor calls, transcripts, recordings, AI timing, provider status, logs and health from one protected dashboard.

### AI Providers

Use OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM and future local models through a provider-oriented architecture.

### Automation

Prepare workflows for n8n, calendar integrations, email drafts and future webhooks.

### Privacy

Self-host first. Keep recordings, transcripts, secrets and customer data under your control.
